Hobbies

In love with life and leveling up

I was born in Cyprus but grew up in Athens. I have Italian roots from Florence. I love programming, reading books, cooking, playing the harmonica and learning new skills.

Passions

Running

I enjoy this solitary meditative activity and I know I can always fall back to if I want to improve my mood. I am quite particular on how much I want to have run in a week and I will always find some time to do so. Though not quite ready yet (can you ever trully be ready?) I have already registered to run my first ultramarathon in 2024 (this still remains to be done).

Scuba diving

I discovered scuba diving while in Cyprus and within a year as my ambitions and love for the sport grew, I begun diving with Global Underwater Explorers. I got my Doubles, Drysuit and DPV certification and participated in a ghost-net retrieval projects with the Ghost Diving team. I completed the GUE Cave diver 1 course in Sardinia with Andrea Marassich and the GUE Cave 2 course in Mexico with JP Bresser.

Freediving

I was also passionate about freediving for a period but stopped just before getting my AIDA 3 certification, due to setbacks with surgeries. As an alternative I really got into Static Apnea training which I try to do regularly during the week as an alternative to meditation. Beeing able to reduce the heart rate and eliminate stress is very important in todays frantic society.

Skydiving

I got my A-licence in Bovec Slovenia on July 2023 and I am hooked on the sport. The plan is to get my B license soon and hopefully own my own skydiving rig in the near future. There are so many disciplines in which I see me progressing in the future and I simply cannot wait.

Paragliding

The "cheapest" way to get your feet of the gound (not really is you dive deep enough). I tried tantem speedflying in the past and I fell in love with the sport. I am an APPI-4 paragliding pilor with about 100h of airtime. I just got into thermal flying and XC. I plan to build some good foundations on canopy handling before I move on to speed wings later on and acro. 2026 update: I did a parakite boogie with Gabe (@partytillimpact) this summer and I will definitely get into parakites as well this year. I am finaly getting my speedflying course this september in Switzerland with Benin Kälin after all these yars

Sailing

I started the ISSA Offshore Skipper course with the sole intention of fighting my sea sickness. (For the record: the seasickness didnt go away)
